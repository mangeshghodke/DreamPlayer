Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

1. Introduction

DreamPlayer ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices regarding that information.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to our servers. The App runs entirely on your device.

The App may access the following on your device:

  • Video and audio files — only when you explicitly choose to play them.
  • Network server credentials (WebDAV, Jellyfin, SMB, FTP) — stored locally on your device in encrypted form. Never transmitted to us.
  • Purchase history — managed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not have access to your payment information.

3. Third-Party Services

The App optionally connects to these third-party services only when you choose to use them:

  • TMDB (The Movie Database) — fetches movie/TV metadata (posters, ratings, cast). Only your TMDB API key (entered by you) and the search query are sent. No personal data is shared.
  • OpenSubtitles — searches for and downloads subtitle files. Only the video filename/hash and your OpenSubtitles credentials (if provided) are sent.
  • Your personal media servers (WebDAV, Jellyfin, SMB, FTP, DLNA) — the App connects directly to servers you configure. No data passes through our systems.

These services have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them.

4. Data Storage

  • All app data (settings, bookmarks, server credentials, playback history) is stored locally on your device using iOS standard storage mechanisms (UserDefaults, Keychain, sandbox file system).
  • No data is uploaded to external servers by the App.
  • Server credentials are stored in the iOS Keychain with kSecAttrAccessibleAfterFirstUnlockThisDeviceOnly protection.

5. In-App Purchases

All purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not collect, store, or have access to your credit card information, Apple ID credentials, or payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to all transactions: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
