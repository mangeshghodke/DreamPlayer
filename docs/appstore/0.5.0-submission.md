## 2. App Review Notes

> **What the app does**
> DreamPlayer is a local media player. You play your own video and audio files
> from your device: the Files app, folder bookmarks, WebDAV, FTP/SFTP, Jellyfin,
> DLNA, and SMB/NAS shares via the Files app's "Connect to Server". No account
> is required, no sign-up, and no content is uploaded anywhere.
>
> **Please test in this order**
>
> 1. **Free trial first.** On first launch a 7-day free trial intro appears. Tap
>    through it — all premium features are then unlocked for 7 days with no
>    purchase. You can also play your own files immediately; browsing, playback,
>    subtitles, and every source are free and are not gated.
> 2. **The paywall.** Let the trial expire, or delete and reinstall the app (the
>    trial is stored in the Keychain and will not reset), then tap any gated
>    feature. Examples: playback speed, subtitle appearance settings,
>    "Download to device", or Dolby Vision / HDR playback on a non-HDR device.
> 3. **Purchase.** On the paywall you will see three products:
>    Premium Monthly ₹199/month, Premium Yearly ₹1,499/year, and a one-time
>    Premium Lifetime ₹4,999. Sandbox payments are fine.
> 4. **Restore.** "Restore Purchases" is available both in Settings and on the
>    paywall sheet, and the entitlement restores correctly.
>
> **Gated (premium) features**: playback speed, A-B repeat, sleep timer,
> subtitle appearance settings, online subtitle search, download to device,
> Dolby Vision / HDR10+ / HLG playback, and volume boost.
>
> **What is NOT gated**: browsing your files, all network sources (SMB, WebDAV,
> FTP/SFTP, Jellyfin, DLNA), video playback, subtitle display and styling
> defaults, picture-in-picture, aspect ratio, and chapters.
>
> **Note on HDR**: Dolby Vision and HDR10 passthrough depend on the display
> hardware, so on a non-HDR device those features stay locked. On devices
> without Dolby Vision support the app explains why and suggests alternatives.
>
> **Privacy**: the app has no analytics, no tracking, and no ads. API keys you
> enter (TMDB, TheTVDB, OpenSubtitles) are stored in the device Keychain and
> are never transmitted anywhere other than the respective provider. The 7-day
> trial marker is stored in the Keychain. Media playback is entirely local or
> from the source you configure.

## 4. If App Review rejects — most likely causes

| Likely reason | Fix |
|---|---|
| "IAP not reachable" / unclear functionality | The review notes already list exactly which features are gated and how to reach the paywall. Make sure the trial intro is *not* blocking — the reviewer must be able to dismiss it. |
| Products not showing in the paywall | Confirm ASC product IDs are exactly `dp_premium_monthly_2026`, `dp_premium_yearly_2026`, `dp_premium_lifetime_2026`. `paywall_sheet.dart` queries them by name; a mismatch falls back to placeholder prices. |
| "Subscription details missing" | Each product needs a description, price, and review screenshot in ASC. The two subscriptions must be in the same subscription group. |
| Restore Purchases not found | It exists in Settings and on the paywall sheet. If rejected anyway, mention the exact path in a follow-up reply. |
