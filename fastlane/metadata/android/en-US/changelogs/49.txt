- Hardware SDR tone-mapping (Settings > Player) now also works for plain
  HDR10 on Qualcomm phones: the video renders into a TextureView while the
  setting is on, which makes the decoder perform the on-chip HDR-to-SDR
  conversion (previously only Dolby Vision was affected). The request is
  never sent to 8-bit H.264, which could freeze the picture.
